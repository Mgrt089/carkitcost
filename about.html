About CarKitCost

CarKitCost is an independently run site built around one question: what does a car actually need for the specific trip you're taking — not a generic "road trip essentials" list that's the same for a weekend drive and a two-week camping trip.

Why this site exists

Most packing content treats every road trip the same. In practice, a beach trip needs sand and water control, a cross-country drive needs backup power and fatigue management, and a weekend trip barely needs any of that. CarKitCost's planner asks for your trip type, vehicle, and trip length, then recommends only the gear that's actually relevant — flagging what's essential versus a nice-to-have upgrade.

How recommendations are chosen

Every item in the planner and guides is selected because it solves a specific, recurring problem for a specific trip type — not because it's a popular or trending product. Each recommendation includes a plain-language reason ("why it fits") so you can judge for yourself whether it applies to your situation, rather than taking it on faith.

How the site is funded

CarKitCost is supported through the Amazon Associates program — when you click a "View picks" link and make a qualifying purchase, the site may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This doesn't change which products get recommended; see our affiliate disclosure for the full policy.

Questions or feedback

If something in a guide is outdated, a recommendation doesn't hold up, or you have a trip scenario the planner doesn't cover well, get in touch on the contact page — that feedback directly shapes what gets updated.
