Editorial policy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains how CarKitCost picks products, checks facts, earns money and fixes mistakes, so you can decide how much to trust what you read here.

Who runs CarKitCost

CarKitCost is independently run. The guides and checklists are written for this site and updated when something changes. See the about page for more.

How we choose products

Each item is chosen because it solves a specific, recurring problem on a specific kind of trip, and each one comes with a short reason it fits. We do not accept payment from brands to feature a product, and commissions do not decide what we recommend.

How we check facts

Advice that affects safety, such as tire pressure, pets in cars, winter driving and carbon monoxide, is checked against authoritative sources, and we list them in a Sources section at the bottom of the page. We link only to pages we have read. If we cannot confirm a figure from a source, we leave the figure out.

What we have and have not tested

Our guides draw on published guidance from safety agencies, manufacturers and trusted organizations. We do not claim hands-on testing of a product unless a page says so.

Affiliate links and prices

CarKitCost is part of the Amazon Associates program, so we may earn a commission when you buy through a link, at no extra cost to you. Links open an Amazon search, and we do not show prices. Read the full affiliate disclosure.

Corrections and updates

If you find a mistake, email hello@carkitcost.com or use the contact page. We correct confirmed errors promptly. Every article shows the date it was published. We add an "Updated" date only when we make a substantive change, such as a new section or corrected advice. Fixing a typo or a layout problem does not change it.
