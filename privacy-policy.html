Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what data CarKitCost (the "site") collects when you use the car setup planner and guide pages, and how that data is used.

Information we collect

CarKitCost does not require account creation or ask for personal information to use the planner or checklist tools. If analytics tracking is enabled, we may collect standard, non-identifying usage data such as pages visited, approximate location (country/region level), device type, and referral source, typically through a tool such as Google Analytics or a privacy-focused alternative.

Cookies

The site may use cookies for basic analytics and to remember on-page choices (such as trip type selections) within your browser session. These cookies don't identify you personally.

Affiliate links

CarKitCost participates in the Amazon Associates program. When you click a "View picks" link and make a qualifying purchase on Amazon, CarKitCost may earn a commission. Amazon's own privacy policy governs any data collected once you leave this site — see their site for details. See our affiliate disclosure for more.

Third-party services

Pages on this site may load fonts from Google Fonts and, where enabled, analytics scripts from a third-party provider. These services may collect standard technical data (such as IP address) as part of normal web delivery.

Data retention & your choices

Since no personal account data is collected by CarKitCost directly, there's no personal profile to request or delete. You can clear cookies or use private browsing to prevent on-site preference storage, and use browser or ad-blocker settings to opt out of third-party analytics.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site's tools or analytics setup change. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact page.
