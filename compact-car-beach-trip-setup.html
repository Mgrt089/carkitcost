Compact car · Beach checklist

Beach trip gear that fits a compact car's limited space.

A compact car makes sand and water control more urgent, not less — there's no spare cargo area to absorb a wet towel pile or a sandy trunk liner problem.

The essentials for this setup

  • Waterproof cargo liner — the single highest-impact item; keeps sand and salt water out of a small carpet area you can't just avoid using.
  • Portable rinse kit — removes sand before people and gear get back into a tight cabin.
  • Dry bag — separates wet gear from everything else when there's no extra trunk zone to dedicate to it.
  • Collapsible trunk organizer — folds flat when empty, useful in a car that can't spare permanent bins.

Why a compact car changes the checklist

In an SUV, a sandy towel is an annoyance. In a compact car, it's touching your seat, your bag, and your floor mats within minutes. Prioritize containment (liner, rinse kit, dry bag) over storage volume — you don't have volume to spare anyway.
